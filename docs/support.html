Frequently Asked Questions

Is The Scriptwriter really free?

Yes, completely. No subscription, no in-app purchases, no account required. The app is free to use forever.

Where are my scripts saved?

Scripts are saved locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server. On the web app, scripts persist in your browser's local storage. You can also save to a file and keep it wherever you like.

Can I open scripts from Final Draft or other editors?

Yes. The Scriptwriter supports importing and exporting Final Draft (.fdx) and Fountain (.fountain) files. Use the File menu to import or export.

How do I export a PDF?

Open the File menu and choose Export PDF. On iPhone, this opens the system share sheet where you can save, print, or send the file.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. The Scriptwriter is a Progressive Web App and works fully offline once loaded. The iOS app also works without an internet connection.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Email us at thescriptwriterapp@gmail.com with a description of what happened. Screenshots or steps to reproduce are always helpful.
